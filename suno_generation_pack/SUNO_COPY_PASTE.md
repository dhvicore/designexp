Math check: https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.12.E1 . For real m,n>0,
B(m,n)=integral from 0 to 1 of x^(m-1)*(1-x)^(n-1) dx
=Gamma(m)*Gamma(n)/Gamma(m+n), symmetric in m,n.
For positive integers, B(m,n)=(m-1)!*(n-1)!/(m+n-1)!.
Rolle: continuous on [a,b], differentiable on (a,b), f(a)=f(b),
there exists c strictly inside (a,b) with f'(c)=0.
